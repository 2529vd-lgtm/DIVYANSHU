**Q1.** Which of the following Fundamental Rights is available **only to citizens** of India?

1. Article 14
2. Article 15
3. Article 21
4. Article 25

<details><summary>Answer dekhein</summary>

**Answer: (2) Article 15.** Articles 15, 16, 19, 29 and 30 are available only to citizens. Articles 14, 21 and 25 are available to all persons.

</details>

**Q2.** Consider the following statements:

1. Article 32 is itself a Fundamental Right.
2. Only the Supreme Court can issue writs.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2

<details><summary>Answer dekhein</summary>

**Answer: (a) 1 only.** Article 32 is a Fundamental Right. High Courts can also issue writs under Article 226.

</details>
