My Daily Study Routine for Competitive Exams

6 Oct 2026 · Exam Prep

Consistency beats intensity. Here's the routine I follow to balance the static syllabus, current affairs and answer writing. Edit this to describe your own routine.

Morning (fresh mind)

  • Static subjects: Polity, History, Geography (about 3 hours)
  • Short break every 50 minutes

Afternoon

  • Newspaper and current affairs notes (1–1.5 hours)
  • Economy / Science & Tech (2 hours)

Evening

  • Answer writing practice: 2–3 questions
  • MCQ practice and revising the day's notes
Tip: revise each topic on day 1, day 7 and day 30 so it moves into long-term memory.
